# AI-Powered Coaching

## Turn Your Habit Data Into Personalized Guidance

Connect Inertium to Claude via MCP and get an AI coach that actually knows your history.

**What this enables:**
- Weekly check-ins that pull your real data automatically
- Progress analysis against personalized projections
- Pattern detection across weeks of entries (GI issues, fatigue signals, plateaus)
- Recommendations grounded in what you actually did, not what you said you did

**Example workflow:**
> "Weekly check-in"
> 
> Claude pulls your weight entries, lift logs, cardio sessions, and notes from Inertium—then compares against your targets and flags what matters.

No manual data entry. No forgetting what you logged. Just show up and get coached.

**Built for:**
- Strength training programs (5x5, PPL, 5/3/1)
- Body recomposition cuts and bulks
- Any habit-tracked fitness goal
